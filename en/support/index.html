Quick answers

The game won't run in the browser?

Try updating your browser and reloading the game page. Close heavy tabs and make sure graphics acceleration is on. Some experiences need special features such as WebGPU.

Do the mobile games need a download?

The games in the mobile section are browser versions with touch controls. Open the game's link in your device's browser. The experience may vary by device.

How do I play your Meta Quest games?

Dahrooj and Mafateeh on Meta Quest are separate from the web versions. You'll find each game's details in the Meta Quest section of the games page, with its store link when available. Mafateeh can also be played free in your browser.

How do I describe a problem helpfully?

Mention the game, the device, the system or browser version, and the steps that led to the problem. If it keeps happening, say when it appears and what you expected to happen.

Before you start

Say which version you're playing: web, mobile browser or Meta Quest. It helps us understand the problem.
