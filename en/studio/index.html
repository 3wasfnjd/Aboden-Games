OUR STORY / THE ABODEN GAMES STORY

A studio the size of a family.
And ideas as big as imagination.

At home, every game we make starts with a chat:
“What if we made a game with…?”

And just like that, the ideas start flowing! Nasser, Joud and Najd each bring their own: one wants to add something, another wants to change something, and each of them imagines the game their own way.

And me, Aboden, their dad. My job is to turn all that chatter into a game they can actually play. I sit down, design and code, with AI helping me, until their idea starts to appear in front of me on the screen.

And whenever something works, I call them: “Come and try it!”

That's when it gets serious. They play and give me their notes, and I find out that what I understood isn't always what they meant. And Najd doesn't bother explaining — she tells me straight:
“What is this? Use your brain!”

So back I go to fix it… what can I do? I have to keep everyone happy!

But it's all worth it when I see them hooked, and when I say “OK, let's stop”, they say:
“Daddy, we just started!”

That's the Aboden Games story. My kids imagine, I try to build what's in their heads, and we test and tweak together. In the end we said: since we're having so much fun, why not let you play with us?
