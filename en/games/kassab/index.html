ABOUT THE GAME

Dunes and rescues.
And a full day every ten minutes.

Dune-bash a pickup across natural dunes, leaving dust and tyre tracks behind you and the other vehicles. The world holds a Najdi quarter with a market and shops, an oasis and a farm, a goat-hair tent and a Bedouin camp.

A full day passes every ten minutes, and at night the car lights, lanterns and tents come on. The weather changes on its own: clear, dust, cloudy, sandstorm and rain. When it rains the valley floods and its water gathers at the farm.

And there are rescues on the way: a water truck stuck in the sand that you pull out with a rope, and a pickup that drives back and forth on the road. People, camels and sheep move around you and keep clear of the car, and there's a radio with four songs.
